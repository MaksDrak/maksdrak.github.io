Whitewater kayaking, skiing, and reflecting on the world.

When I'm not analyzing data at a computer, I'm usually outside, admiring nature and soaking up its benefits. I love the adrenaline of jumping waterfalls, as well as the raw beauty of mountain rivers. I love the gothic sharpness of mountain peaks, which invites reflection on the world and our place in it.

🛶

Kayaking +

I've been a kayaker for as long as I can remember — I started paddling at age 6. This sport helps me make decisions quickly, under the pressure of time (and water) running out. It also teaches me trust and humility toward the people I work with, and toward an element that often can't be fought, only listened to.
⛷️

Skiing +

I'm a certified alpine ski instructor, and this sport always teaches me humility and patience in the face of new challenges. What I value most about it is how much creativity it demands in taking an individual approach to every person or group.
📚

Philosophy +

I'm interested in phenomenology and environmental philosophy. I try to spread a sense of wonder at what simply is, at what we have in our experience, and to explore the thinking of the greatest philosophers by reading them anew.
🌲

Nature +

Time in nature gives me the energy and strength to make sense of everyday reality. Nature is an enormous heritage that we have to protect, and doing so is one of my life's missions.
